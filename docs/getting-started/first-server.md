# Create Your First Server

Once your Arlo account is ready and you have enough Arlo Credits, you can create your first hosting server.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- An active Arlo account
- Enough Arlo Credits for your chosen hosting plan
- A hosting plan selected

## Choose a hosting plan

Arlo Hosting offers several plans with different resource limits.

When choosing a plan, consider what your application needs, including:

- RAM
- CPU
- Storage
- Databases
- Backups

You can view the available plans and their current pricing through the Arlo Hosting dashboard.

## Create your server

To create a server:

1. Sign in to your **[Arlo Hosting dashboard](https://dash.arlostudios.dev)**.
2. Open the Servers section.
3. Select **Create Server**
4. Fill in the necessary details and choose your hosting plan.
5. Review the resources and pricing.
6. Confirm your order.

Once your order has been processed, Arlo will automatically provision your server.

:::info
Server provisioning is enabled. Your server may take a short time to become available after your order is confirmed.
:::

## Access your server

Once provisioning is complete, your server will appear in your Arlo Hosting Dashboard.

From there, you can access the **[Arlo Hosting Panel](https://panel.arlostudios.dev)** to manage your server.

:::info
Your Arlo Hosting Panel login uses the **same credentials as your Arlo dashboard account**. You do not need to create a seperate Panel account.
:::

The panel allows you to:

- Start, stop, and restart your server
- View the console
- View audit logs
- Manage files
- Manage databases
- Manage backups
- Monitor resource usage

:::tip
If your server does not appear after provisioning has completed, contact Arlo Support and include your order or service details.
:::
