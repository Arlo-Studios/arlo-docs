# Welcome to Arlo Hosting

Welcome to **Arlo Hosting**.

Arlo Hosting provides reliable, straightforward hosting for Discord bots and other applications, giving you the tools you need to deploy and manage your services without having to manage the underlying infrastructure yourself.

Whether you're hosting your first Discord bot or already know your way around a server, these docs are here to help.

## Getting started

If you're new to Arlo Hosting, we recommend following these steps:

1. **Create your Arlo account**
2. **Get Arlo Credits**
3. **Choose a hosting plan**
4. **Create your server**
5. **Deploy your application**
6. **Start your server**

Once your server is running, you can manage it through the **Arlo Hosting Panel**.

## What can you do?

With Arlo Hosting, you can:

- Manage your hosted services from the Arlo Hosting Panel
- Start, stop, and restart your server
- Upload and manage files
- View your server console and logs
- Manage databases
- Create and manage backups
- Host Discord bots and other supported applications
- Monitor your service and its resource usage

## Need help?

If something isn't working as expected, check the **Troubleshooting** section first.

If you can't find an answer in the documentation, you can contact **Arlo Support** for assistance.

::: tip
Keeping your bot's files, configuration, and credentials organised will make troubleshooting much easier if something goes wrong.
:::
