# Using the Panel

The **Arlo Hosting Panel** is where you manage your Arlo Hosting servers.

From the panel, you can control your server, manage files, view your console, and access other tools available with your hosting plan.

## Accessing the Panel

You can access the Arlo Hosting Panel at: **[panel.arlostudios.dev](https://panel.arlostudios.dev)**

Your panel login uses the **same credentials as your Arlo dashboard account**. You do not need to create a seperate Panel account.

## Your server

After signing in, you'll be able to view the servers associated with your account.

Selecting a server will open its management page.

From here, you can access the tools available for that server.

## Server management

The Panel provides controls for managing your server, including:

- Starting your server
- Stopping your server
- Restarting your server
- Viewing your server console
- Managing server files
- Managing databases
- Managing backups
- Viewing resource usage

The exact features available may depend on your hosting plan.

:::tip
If you're unsure about where to find something, start from your server's management page. The available tools and controls are organised there.
:::

## Keeping your account secure

Your Arlo account gives access to your hosting services, so keep your login details secure.

::: warning
Never share your Arlo account password with anyone. Arlo Support will never ask you for your password.
:::
